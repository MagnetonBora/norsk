## 📝 Norwegian Grammar Summary: Verb Forms

The main challenge when using two verbs together in Norwegian is deciding whether to use the infinitive marker **"å"** (to) before the second verb.

---

### 1. Infinitive WITHOUT "å" (Modal Verbs)

Do **not** use **"å"** before the second verb when the first verb in the sentence is a **modal verb**. The second verb must be in the **infinitive form** (base form).

|**Modal Verbs (Hjelpeverb)**|**Example (Correct)**|**Example (Incorrect)**|
|---|---|---|
|**kan** (can/able to)|Maria **kan lage** middag.|Maria kan **å lage** middag.|
|**skal** (shall/will)|Jeg **skal kjøpe** mat.|Jeg skal **å kjøpe** mat.|
|**vil** (will/want to)|De **vil snakke** norsk.|De vil **å snakke** norsk.|
|**må** (must)|Du **må sove** nå.|Du må **å sove** nå.|
|**bør** (should)|Vi **bør trene** mer.|Vi bør **å trene** mer.|

---

### 2. Infinitive WITH "å" (Standard Verbs)

You **must** use **"å"** before the second verb when the first verb is a **standard (non-modal) verb** that expresses an action or feeling.

|**Standard Verbs**|**Example (Correct)**|**Example (Incorrect)**|
|---|---|---|
|**begynner** (starts)|Tor **begynner å arbeide**.|Tor begynner arbeide.|
|**liker** (likes)|Bartek **liker å ta** toget.|Bartek liker ta toget.|
|**prøver** (tries)|Hun **prøver å snakke** spansk.|Hun prøver snakke spansk.|
|**slutter** (stops)|Jeg **slutter å røyke**.|Jeg slutter røyke.|
